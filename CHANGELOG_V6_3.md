# BorderWise AI v6.3

## User-defined student scenario
- Semester tuition is now user-entered instead of hard-coded.
- Scholarship and loan coverage are separate inputs; forecast uses net tuition.
- Tuition due date is user-entered and only enters the 30-day forecast when due within 30 days.
- Accommodation is user-entered monthly.
- Monthly spending categories are user-entered and drive the spending analysis and cash-flow forecast.
- Current MYR/SGD balances, income and emergency reserve remain user-editable.
- Spending dashboard is now explicitly based on a manual monthly plan, not a handful of demo transactions.
- Demo defaults use S$9,025 semester tuition, matching the scenario supplied for the competition demo, but every value can be changed.
