Build your student financial profile

Currency comes first. Choose the currency you want XKF5 to use for planning and reporting. You can still hold money in many currencies and convert between any supported pair. XKF5 does not connect to a bank account in this sandbox.

1. Your currencies & FX

Planning currency is the currency used for your cash-flow outlook, spending plan and recommendations. It does not restrict which currencies you can hold or convert.
Choose any supported ISO 4217 currency, e.g. SGD, MYR, USD or CNY. This is only your planning/reporting currency.
Add every currency you actually hold. Auto FX is the default.
For each balance, choose Auto current reference or My quoted rate. Auto mode retrieves the current reference rate; quoted mode lets you enter a bank or money-changer quote. Rates are shown in the wallet with source/date.

2. Current money, income & reserve

XKF5 protects the equivalent value; this is not a bank guarantee.

3. Education funding

Enter the gross tuition for one semester. Scholarship and loan coverage are deducted to derive the net amount XKF5 plans for. Tuition can be in any currency you select.
